## Done

- **Shared areas:** `xml`, `opc`, `units`, `color`, `geometry`, `drawingml`, `diagram` (SmartArt), `chart` (model, parser, writer and SVG painter), `digest`, `crypto` (package encryption), `math`, `text`, `i18n`, `ribbon`, `collab` (Yjs real-time collaboration), `teams`, `automation` and `embedding`. `AGENTS.md` has the authoritative list.
- **Formats as areas:** `docx`, `xlsx`, `pptx` and `visio`, each with its own parser and writer (Visio edits are bounded, and legacy `.vsd` is preview only).
- **Products:** the PowerPoint, Word, Excel, Visio and OpenTeams viewers and the shared `ooxml-ui` elements live in this repository and are released from one pipeline.
- **PowerPoint viewer moved in (2026-11-16):** pptx-viewer lives in `viewers/pptx` with its history and release tags, its demos and browser tests are at `demos/pptx/` and `e2e/pptx/`, and CI verifies it like the other viewers. With no outside repository consuming this one, the sync workflow, the local-linking script and the fleet report are retired.
- **Wave 6 (2026-10-23):** one binding kit in `ooxml-ui/bindings`, pptx headless controllers, and chart and SmartArt editing shared across products. Word and Excel insert and edit SmartArt; Word, Excel and Visio edit charts; pptx saves classic chart title, legend, axis and series edits and ChartEx title, legend, label and series edits through the neutral writers. Details are in [structure-plan.md](structure-plan.md) section 5 and [agnostic-core-plan.md](agnostic-core-plan.md).
- **Shared Office delivery (2026-10-27):** source-backed chart admission and saves, interpreted Word/Excel SmartArt previews and generated 2D caches, shared SmartArt style/colour galleries, DrawingML outlines/effects/format schemes, advanced shared chart controls, and Word/Excel ChartEx editing. PowerPoint's admitted classic and ChartEx save families and shared model projections have corpus, browser and native reopen evidence. The [delivery ledger](office-shared-parity-delivery.md) records tested cases and remaining boundaries.

## Next

- **Finish the DrawingML consumer migration:** shared outlines, typed effect lists, theme format schemes with `phClr` substitution and pattern/picture fill codecs are implemented. The pptx colour codec (all 26 transforms, preset and system colours), the theme part reader, the format scheme reader, `fillRef`/`lnRef`/`effectRef` resolution with `phClr`, the master `p:clrMap` reader and the theme writer now run on the shared resolver, `parseTheme`, `parseThemeFormatScheme`, the `resolveTheme*Style` resolvers and `drawingThemeXml`, pinned by corpus parity harnesses (2026-11-16); colour opacity and the UI's colour-choice reader use the resolver's alpha and the core reader, the gradient and effect-list codecs read through the shared readers, and effect DAGs run on a shared model and interpretation (sibling composition, masks, blends and transforms are still approximated).
