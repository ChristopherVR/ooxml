## Breaking

- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: `isEditorControlTarget` and the `isControlTarget` flag of `EditorKeyGuard` are gone. The keymap now takes `slideFocus` (`'outside' | 'pane' | 'objects'`), which `slideKeyFocus(target)` computes for you, and `dispatchEditorKey` fills it in when you omit it.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: the keymap has a new action, `editText` (Enter on a selected object inside the slide). Code that switches over every `EditorKeyActionName` needs a case for it, and `EditorKeyHandlers` takes an `editText` handler.
- PowerPoint viewers: Tab no longer selects slide objects from anywhere in the viewer. Press Enter on the slide, press F6 to reach it, or click it first. From then on Tab and Shift+Tab walk the objects as before.
- `pptx-react-viewer`, `@christophervr/pptx-viewer`, `pptx-vue-viewer` and `pptx-angular-viewer`: Tab and Shift+Tab in the speaker notes no longer indent and outdent a paragraph. They move focus on, as in the vanilla and Svelte viewers. Use the notes toolbar's Increase and Decrease Indent buttons instead.

## Migration

- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: if you called `mapEditorKey` with `isControlTarget`, pass `slideFocus: slideKeyFocus(event.target)` instead. To let Tab walk objects in your own surface, mark its container with `data-pptx-viewport` and attach `attachViewportNavigation`.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: to support Enter on a selected object in your own surface, pass `editText: () => editSlideObjectText(document, selectedId)` to `dispatchEditorKey`. It opens the object as a double-click does.
- PowerPoint viewers: end-to-end tests that pressed Tab straight after loading a deck should press Enter on the slide first.

## Added

- PowerPoint viewers: F6 and Shift+F6 move between the ribbon, slide, format pane, notes, status bar and slide list, as in PowerPoint. The keyboard shortcut reference lists the new keys in every language, including the demo apps.
- PowerPoint viewers: Enter on a selected object starts editing its text, as in PowerPoint. Escape ends the edit and keeps focus on the slide.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: `handleNotesFieldKey` gives a speaker-notes field the shared keys (Escape back to the slide, Ctrl+Enter to commit), and `editSlideObjectText` opens a selected object for editing.
- `ooxml-ui/docx` and Word viewers: `<docx-editor>` has `getTextSelection()` and `focusText(range)`, so a host that reloads a document can put the user's caret back.
- Office suite: a Settings option turns off the `/` shortcut that jumps to search.

## Fixed

- `ooxml-ui`, Word viewers, Excel viewers, Visio viewers, PowerPoint viewers and Office suite: Tab and Shift+Tab can no longer leave `office-ui-dialog`, Escape closes it wherever focus has ended up, and focus returns to the control that opened it, including inside shadow roots. This covers the Word, Excel, Visio and suite dialogs, and PowerPoint Options.
- `pptx-react-viewer` and `@christophervr/pptx-viewer`: moving to another slide no longer drops keyboard focus to the page, and the slide stays a Tab stop.
- `pptx-react-viewer`, `@christophervr/pptx-viewer`, `pptx-vue-viewer` and `pptx-angular-viewer`: the speaker notes no longer trap the keyboard. Tab, Shift+Tab, F6 and Shift+F6 leave the field, and Escape returns to the slide, in every binding.
- `pptx-react-viewer`, `@christophervr/pptx-viewer`, `pptx-vue-viewer`, `pptx-angular-viewer` and `pptx-svelte-viewer`: ending text editing with Escape or Ctrl+Enter returns focus to the slide, as the vanilla viewer does, so Tab, Delete and the other shortcuts keep working.
- PowerPoint viewers: keys pressed in a dialog no longer reach the editor beneath it, so Delete on a dialog button no longer deletes the selected object.
- `pptx-react-viewer`, `@christophervr/pptx-viewer`, `pptx-svelte-viewer` and `pptx-vanilla-viewer`: the hyperlink dialog takes focus, keeps Tab inside, closes on Escape and returns focus to where it was opened.
- PowerPoint viewers: the File tab moves focus into the backstage and keeps it there, and Escape returns to the ribbon.
- `ooxml-ui/xlsx` and Excel viewers: Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown switch sheets instead of moving the active cell, and the grid then announces the cell on the new sheet.
- `ooxml-ui/xlsx` and Excel viewers: F6 and Shift+F6 move through every part of the window, including the status bar, and no longer stop on the sheet tabs or the ribbon.
- `ooxml-ui/visio` and Visio viewers: a shape keeps keyboard focus when the drawing redraws, so arrow nudges can be repeated and Delete still works. Focus moves to the canvas when the shape is gone.
- Office suite: saving and exporting keep keyboard focus and the selection in the document, in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Visio.
- Office suite: applying or undoing an assistant edit keeps keyboard focus on the assistant card and keeps the caret, active cell, slide or page in the document.
- Office suite: text typed with an input method while a document is being saved or changed by the assistant is kept, and the tab shows it as unsaved until it is saved.

## Accessibility

- PowerPoint viewers: the slide is a single named Tab stop, so the keyboard can always move past it. Its focus ring shows when the keyboard reaches it, not after a mouse click. Escape clears the selection and then leaves the slide, and a screen reader hears the name of each object Tab reaches.
- `ooxml-ui/visio` and Visio viewers: group shapes show a focus ring, and connectors describe the shapes they join.
- Office suite: an assistant reply is announced when it starts and when it ends, not word by word, and chat messages are labelled by who wrote them.
- Office suite: in shared chats, messages from other people and finished replies are announced to screen readers. Joining a chat does not read out its history.
- Office suite: the page, the editors and Teams take `lang` and `dir` from the display language, while the workspace chrome, which is English, stays marked as English.
- Office suite: the document tabs form a valid tab list, the phone Assistant button has a name, and a save is announced once.
- `ooxml-ui/docx` and Word viewers: the Font and Size boxes expose their expanded state and the list they control.
