## Breaking

- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: `isEditorControlTarget` and the `isControlTarget` flag of `EditorKeyGuard` are gone. The keymap now takes `slideFocus` (`'outside' | 'pane' | 'objects'`), which `slideKeyFocus(target)` computes for you, and `dispatchEditorKey` fills it in when you omit it.
- PowerPoint viewers (React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, vanilla): Tab no longer selects slide objects from anywhere in the viewer. Press Enter on the slide, press F6 to reach it, or click it first. From then on Tab and Shift+Tab walk the objects as before.

## Migration

- If you called `mapEditorKey` with `isControlTarget`, pass `slideFocus: slideKeyFocus(event.target)` instead. To let Tab walk objects in your own surface, mark its container with `data-pptx-viewport` and attach `attachViewportNavigation`.
- End-to-end tests that pressed Tab straight after loading a deck should press Enter on the slide first.

## Added

- PowerPoint viewers: F6 and Shift+F6 move between the ribbon, slide, format pane, notes, status bar and slide list, as in PowerPoint. The keyboard shortcut reference lists the new keys in every language.
- Office suite: a Settings option turns off the `/` shortcut that jumps to search.

## Fixed

- `ooxml-ui`: Tab and Shift+Tab can no longer leave `office-ui-dialog`, Escape closes it wherever focus has ended up, and focus returns to the control that opened it, including inside shadow roots. This covers Word, Excel, Visio and the suite's dialogs, and PowerPoint Options.
- Office suite: saving, exporting and applying an AI change keep keyboard focus and the selection in the document, in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Visio.
- Excel: Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown switch sheets instead of moving the active cell.
- Visio: a shape keeps keyboard focus when the drawing redraws, so arrow nudges can be repeated and Delete still works. Focus moves to the canvas when the shape is gone.

## Accessibility

- PowerPoint viewers: the slide is a single named Tab stop with a visible focus ring, so the keyboard can always move past it. Escape clears the selection and then leaves the slide, and a screen reader hears the name of each object Tab reaches.
- Visio: group shapes show a focus ring, and connectors describe the shapes they join.
- Office suite: an assistant reply is announced when it starts and when it ends, not word by word, and chat messages are labelled by who wrote them.
- Office suite: the page, the editors and Teams take `lang` and `dir` from the display language, while the workspace chrome, which is English, stays marked as English.
- Office suite: the document tabs form a valid tab list, the phone Assistant button has a name, and a save is announced once.
- Word: the Font and Size boxes expose their expanded state and the list they control.
