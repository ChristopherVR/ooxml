## Accessibility

- `ooxml-ui`, PowerPoint viewers and Office suite: every control's accessible name now contains the text it shows (WCAG 2.5.3, Label in Name), so speech input users can say what they see. Captions, tooltips and icons stay as Microsoft Office shows them. Only names changed.
- `ooxml-ui` and PowerPoint viewers: the status bar's zoom percentage button is named by the percentage it shows, then what it does, for example "100%, Zoom to fit". This covers `office-ui-status-bar`, `pptx-ui-status-bar` and the Svelte viewer toolbar. The new `zoomFitName` helper, exported from `ooxml-ui` and `ooxml-ui/pptx`, builds that name.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx` and PowerPoint viewers: the Text Shadow and Character Spacing icons draw their bold letters as outlines instead of text, so they look as before and the buttons are named only by their commands.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx`, PowerPoint viewers and Office suite: Design > Fonts tiles are named by what they show, then the scheme, for example "Aa Aptos Display / Aptos: Office". Their tooltips keep PowerPoint's wording ("Office: Aptos Display / Aptos"). The names come from the new `pptx.gallery.themeFonts.schemeName` and `pptx.gallery.themeFonts.currentName` keys, and `pptx.gallery.themeFonts.scheme` and `pptx.gallery.themeFonts.current` now give only the tooltips.
- `pptx-svelte-viewer` and `pptx-angular-viewer`: the Svelte "+ Show" button is named by its caption, as in the other bindings, and the Angular custom shows button is named by its caption, "Show".
- `ooxml-ui/xlsx` and Excel viewers: the formula bar's Insert Function button keeps its name, and its fx glyph is now an icon rather than text.
- `ooxml-ui/docx` and Word viewers: Word ribbon commands whose caption is shorter than their command name have their own accessible names in every locale. For example, Insert > Pictures is named "Insert pictures" ("Bilder einfügen", "Insérer des images", "Insertar imágenes", "插入图片"). Dialogs, menus and tooltips keep their wording.
- `ooxml-ui/visio` and Visio viewers: gallery buttons such as Design > Backgrounds, Borders & Titles, Re-Layout Page and Insert > Callout show their caption in the display language, matching their names. The More button of an inline gallery is named in that language too, for example "Weitere Varianten".
- Office suite: the account button is named by the profile, with the initials drawn as a picture, and the workspace switcher is named by the workspace it shows, with "Switch workspace" as its tooltip.

## Changed

- PowerPoint viewers: the French Notes title is "Notes", as PowerPoint shows it, matching the Notes button.
- `ooxml-ui` and PowerPoint viewers: hosts or tests that find the zoom percentage button by the name "Zoom to fit" should match the new name, which starts with the percentage, or select `[data-id="zoomFit"]`.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx` and PowerPoint viewers: hosts that translate the Design > Fonts tiles should add `pptx.gallery.themeFonts.schemeName` and `pptx.gallery.themeFonts.currentName`, starting with `Aa {{major}} / {{minor}}`, the text the tile shows. `RibbonGalleryItem` and `OfficeGalleryItem` take an optional tooltip (`titleKey` and `title`, or `title`) for tiles whose tooltip differs from their name.
