## Accessibility

- Every control's accessible name now contains the text it shows (WCAG 2.5.3, Label in Name), so speech input users can say what they see. This covers the OOXML Office suite, `ooxml-ui` and every PowerPoint binding.
- `ooxml-ui` status bar: the zoom percentage button is named by the percentage it shows, then what it does, for example "100%, Zoom to fit", in `office-ui-status-bar`, `pptx-ui-status-bar` and the Svelte viewer toolbar. The new `zoomFitName` helper (exported from `ooxml-ui` and `ooxml-ui/pptx`) builds that name.
- `ooxml-ui/pptx`: the Text Shadow and Character Spacing icons are drawn as shapes instead of letters, so the buttons are named only by their commands.
- `ooxml-ui/xlsx`: the formula bar's Insert Function button keeps its name, and its fx glyph is now an icon rather than text.
- `ooxml-ui/docx`: Word ribbon names now contain their captions in every locale. The Insert tab's picture command is captioned Picture, and Different odd and even pages shows its full name. French, German, Spanish and Chinese section break, table, picture, view and selection names were reworded to contain their captions (for example "Abschnittswechsel: nächste Seite" and "Alles markieren").
- OOXML Office: the account button is named by the profile, with the initials drawn as a picture, and the workspace switcher is named by the workspace it shows, with "Switch workspace" as its tooltip.

## Changed

- `pptx-viewer-locales`: the French Notes pane title is "Notes", matching the Notes button.
- `ooxml-ui`: hosts or tests that find the zoom percentage button by the name "Zoom to fit" should match the new name, which starts with the percentage, or select `[data-id="zoomFit"]`.
