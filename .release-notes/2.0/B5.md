## Added

- `ooxml-core/docx`: `InlineImage.textBoxContent` holds a text box's `w:txbxContent` as real paragraphs and tables, read by the same parser as the document body, with the anchor and insets of its `wps:bodyPr`, for every shape that has a `wps:txbx` whatever its preset. `drawingShapeSvg` draws a drawing's shape without its own text when those paragraphs are drawn over it.

## Changed

- `ooxml-core/docx` and Word viewers: the text inside a drawing shape is editable only when it is the plain centred text the editor writes. Text with bold, sizes, borders or other alignment is read-only, so changing the shape's size, fill or outline no longer rewrites that text as plain centred lines.

## Fixed

- `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: a text box drawn as a shape (a `rect` holding a `wps:txbx`, as letterheads often have) shows its paragraphs with their runs, bold and italic, sizes, colours, alignment and paragraph borders, inset and clipped to the shape over its fill and outline. This holds on the editing surface, in Print Layout and in the header, footer and note previews of Draft view, where such shapes used to show as an empty dashed box or the word "Shape".
- `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: lines and connectors such as `straightConnector1` in headers and footers are drawn with their outline colour, width and dash in Print Layout and Draft view, instead of an empty placeholder box.
