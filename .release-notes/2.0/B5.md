## Added

- `ooxml-core/docx`: `InlineImage.textBoxContent` holds a text box's `w:txbxContent` as real paragraphs and tables, read by the same parser as the document body, with the anchor and insets of its `wps:bodyPr`, for every shape that has a `wps:txbx` whatever its preset. `drawingShapeSvg` draws a drawing's shape without its own text when those paragraphs are drawn over it.
- `ooxml-core/docx`: `ShapeSpec.fontColor` is the colour of a shape's `wps:style` font reference, and `simpleTextBoxDrawing` gives the frame and paragraphs an edited simple text box is saved with. `ooxml-core/docx/layout` adds `shapePaint`, the shape SVG, text box content and default text colour every view draws a shape with.

## Changed

- `ooxml-core/docx` and Word viewers: the text inside a drawing shape is editable only when it is the plain centred text the editor writes. Text with bold, sizes, borders or other alignment is read-only, so changing the shape's size, fill or outline no longer rewrites that text as plain centred lines.
- `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: Draft view's header, footer and note previews leave floating drawings (pictures, shapes and text boxes anchored to the page or paragraph) out, as Word's Draft view does, so a tall letterhead text box no longer stretches the header preview. Inline drawings still show, and Print Layout is unchanged.
- `ooxml-core/docx`, `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: a text box written with `txBox="1"` (Word's Insert > Text Box) is read with its frame and drawn like any shape. Selecting one shows the Shape Format tab; Add Text and Insert > Text Box edit it in the Text Box dialog as before, and an unchanged box is saved byte for byte.

## Fixed

- `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: a text box drawn as a shape (a `rect` holding a `wps:txbx`, as letterheads often have) shows its paragraphs with their runs, bold and italic, sizes, colours, alignment and paragraph borders, inset and clipped to the shape over its fill and outline. This holds on the editing surface, in Print Layout and for inline drawings in Draft view previews, where such shapes used to show as an empty dashed box or the word "Shape".
- `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: lines and connectors such as `straightConnector1` in headers and footers are drawn with their outline colour, width and dash in Print Layout, instead of an empty placeholder box.
- `ooxml-core/docx`, `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: text boxes written with `txBox="1"` are drawn in Print Layout, floating and inline, in the body and in headers and footers, instead of being left out.
- `ooxml-core/docx`, `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: shape text where runs set no colour takes the colour of the shape's `wps:style` font reference, resolved through the theme, as Word draws it, in SVG text and over text box paragraphs alike. Without a font reference it stays white on dark fills and black on light ones. Saving a shape whose text the style colours writes no colour on its runs, so Word shows the same colour after a resize or refill, and text whose runs share one colour keeps it when it is rewritten. A shape with neither a style nor its own fill or outline, an outline that sets only a width, or a style reference with `idx="0"` is drawn without that fill or outline, as Word draws it, instead of in the default blue.
- `ooxml-ui` and Word viewers: pictures in Draft view's header, footer and note previews, and pictures inside text boxes, are drawn as the body draws them, from the same media cache (SVG originals and cropped or recoloured fills included), so a preview re-render creates no new picture URLs, all of them are released with the document, and pictures that arrive late in a collaboration session repaint in previews and text boxes as they do in the body.
