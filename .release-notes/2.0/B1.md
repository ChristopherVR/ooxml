## Added

- `ooxml-core/opc`: `findSharedOwnedParts` lists every part of a package that more than one owner claims, such as a chart named by two graphic frames or a theme named by two masters, and `ownsRelationshipTarget` tells whether a relationship makes its source the owner of its target. `clonePart` copies one part under a fresh name, with its content type and the parts it owns.
- `ooxml-core/pptx`: elements carry `relationshipSourcePath`, the part their relationship ids belong to. Loading sets it, copies keep it, and collaboration shares it.

## Fixed

- PowerPoint viewers and `ooxml-core/pptx`: a duplicated or pasted chart, SmartArt graphic, OLE object, video or audio clip saves with its own relationships, and with its own chart part, embedded workbook, diagram parts or embedded object. Before, two charts could share one chart part and workbook, which PowerPoint refused to open, an edit to one changed the other, and a chart pasted on another slide could show that slide's own chart. Pictures and media files stay shared. This covers duplicate and paste in every binding, the SDK's `duplicateElement`, copies kept by undo history, a move to another slide followed by deleting the source slide, and a collaborator saving the same edits.
- PowerPoint viewers and `ooxml-core/pptx`: Duplicate Slide Master gives the new master its own theme and its own copies of its layouts and their parts, and Insert Slide Master gives the new master its own copy of the first master's theme. Before, both masters named one theme part, which PowerPoint refused to open. New masters and layouts also get ids no other master or layout uses.
- PowerPoint viewers and `ooxml-core/pptx`: Duplicate Layout gives the new layout its own copies of the charts, diagrams and other parts the source layout owns.
