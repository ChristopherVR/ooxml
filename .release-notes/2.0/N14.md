## Performance

- The PowerPoint slide thumbnail rail in `pptx-react-viewer`, `pptx-vue-viewer`, `pptx-svelte-viewer`, `pptx-angular-viewer` and `pptx-vanilla-viewer` reads scrolling once per frame and keeps the thumbnails that stay in view. In a deck of 50 or more slides, thumbnails that scroll into view are drawn once scrolling pauses, so the rail scrolls at full frame rate. Moving to another slide no longer redraws the visible thumbnails.
- In `pptx-react-viewer`, moving to another slide no longer copies and compares the whole deck for the undo history, so paging through a large deck is about twice as fast.

## Fixed

- Paging through slides with the keyboard while the thumbnail rail has focus keeps focus on the current slide's thumbnail, as PowerPoint does. Before, focus was lost once the first thumbnail scrolled out of the rail, and in the Vue, Svelte and vanilla viewers the keys then scrolled the rail instead of changing the slide.

## Added

- `ooxml-ui/pptx` exports the scroll window every binding's thumbnail rail uses: `createVirtualWindow`, `createDeferredRows`, `sameRenderedWindow`, `railHasFocus`, `focusCurrentRow` and their constants.
