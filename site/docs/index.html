Get started / Overview

Documentation

OOXML Office is a set of Office editors that run in the browser: Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Visio files, plus a team workspace.

There are three ways in. Use the apps as they are, embed one editor in your own site, or build on the library underneath them.

Get started

Use the appsOpen a file and work on it. No account, nothing leaves your device. Embed an editorAdd Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Visio or Teams to a React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, Solid or plain page. Build on the libraryRead, change and write the files from code, with no UI, or drive them from an agent.

The app in Simple view

The document first, the ribbon one click away

OOXML Office opens a file under one short bar: its name, Save, undo and redo, bold, italic and underline in Word and Excel, the slide or page in view and, where a deployment turns it on, the assistant. Show ribbon on that bar brings each app's full ribbon back at once, with your edits and undo history as they were; Simple view returns, and Settings, View, Layout keeps your choice. The editor packages you embed in your own site are not affected: they show their full ribbon unless you ask otherwise.
